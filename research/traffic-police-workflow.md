# Traffic Police Workflow Research

## Purpose

Understand how traffic police currently manage manually controlled traffic routes.

## Questions to Study

### Traffic Control
- How many routes are controlled?
- How is the active route selected?
- How long is each route allowed to move?
- How is the next route selected?
- Can the timing change according to traffic conditions?

### Communication
- How do officers communicate with other officers?
- How do drivers currently know which route is open?
- How are sudden changes communicated?
- How are emergency situations handled?

### Operational Challenges
- Driver confusion
- Unexpected traffic buildup
- Long waiting periods
- Manual coordination
- Communication delays
- Emergency vehicle movement

### GoSignal Opportunity

GoSignal could provide a digital communication layer between authorized traffic authorities and drivers.

Potential features:
- Route-specific GO / STOP status
- Live countdown
- Police-controlled updates
- Emergency alerts
- Location-based driver notifications
- Control history and logs

## Field Research

Before any real-world pilot, collect information from authorized traffic personnel and document:

- Current workflow
- Typical route sequence
- Typical timing
- Major problems
- Safety requirements
- Required permissions
- Recommended pilot procedure
