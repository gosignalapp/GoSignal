# Bhiwandi Traffic Problem

## Problem Overview

Bhiwandi and surrounding highway routes can experience significant traffic congestion, especially at locations where traffic is managed manually by traffic police.

## Current Traffic Management Challenge

At some locations, traffic police may control different road directions manually instead of using fixed traffic lights.

This can create a situation where:

- One route is allowed to move.
- Other routes must remain stopped.
- The active route may change after a certain period.
- Drivers may not know in advance when their route will be released.
- Waiting time can be difficult to predict.

## GoSignal Proposed Solution

GoSignal aims to provide a real-time communication system between traffic authorities and drivers.

The system can provide:

- Route-specific GO / STOP status
- Real-time countdown
- GPS-based route identification
- Driver notifications
- Traffic police control interface
- Emergency traffic instructions

## Important Note

GoSignal is a proposed technology solution and must not be treated as an official traffic-control system until it is approved and operated by the relevant traffic authorities.

## Research Required

- Study actual traffic flow at the selected location.
- Identify the number of controlled routes.
- Record typical traffic-control cycles.
- Understand how traffic police currently manage the routes.
- Measure average waiting time.
- Identify safety and operational requirements.
- Identify required permissions for a pilot deployment.
