# Existing Traffic Management Systems

## Purpose

This document studies existing technologies and systems used to manage road traffic.

## Areas to Study

### Navigation Applications
- Google Maps
- Waze
- Other navigation and traffic-information platforms

### Smart Traffic Systems
- Fixed-time traffic signals
- Adaptive traffic signal systems
- Traffic monitoring systems
- Intelligent transportation systems

### Manual Traffic Management
- Traffic police-controlled intersections
- Temporary traffic diversions
- Manual route release systems
- Emergency traffic control

## Key Questions

- How is traffic status collected?
- Who controls the traffic?
- How is information delivered to drivers?
- Is the information real-time?
- Does the system support manually controlled routes?
- Does it provide countdown information?
- Can authorities send route-specific instructions?

## GoSignal Research Goal

Identify gaps in existing systems and determine where GoSignal can provide useful additional functionality.
