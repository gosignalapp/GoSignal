# GoSignal Competitor Analysis

## Purpose

This document studies existing traffic and navigation solutions to identify gaps that GoSignal can address.

## Systems to Study

- Google Maps
- Waze
- Smart Traffic Signal Systems
- Adaptive Traffic Control Systems
- Police-controlled traffic management systems
- Real-time traffic information platforms

## Research Questions

- What problem does each system solve?
- What data does it use?
- Does it provide live traffic-signal information?
- Does it support police-controlled traffic routes?
- Does it provide route-specific GO / STOP instructions?
- Does it provide a live countdown?
- What are its limitations?
- What can GoSignal do differently?
